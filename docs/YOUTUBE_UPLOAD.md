## Title

WallAlive — Drawings Come Alive in AR with WebMCP | Hackathon Demo

## Description

WallAlive turns a child’s approved wall drawing into a mathematically reconstructed, closed 3D volume that lives and performs in the room.

The child explicitly opens the camera and approves one or more drawings. Local segmentation and six ONNX graphs preserve artwork, detect semantic parts, and build separate movement rigs. A visiting browser agent uses eight goal-level WebMCP tools to inspect the shared scene and every rig's verified abilities, request 3D, stage typed multi-character learning stories, direct visible ensemble moments, orchestrate spatial cinematics, prepare a safe merchandise concept, and verify attributed history. A staged story remains inert until the person presses **Approve & play**. The camera, purchase, and approval are deliberately absent from the tool surface.

The learner-facing result is **Imagine → Sequence → Perform → Reflect**: start from a personally meaningful drawing, shape a beginning–middle–end story, perform it, then retell or revise it. Narrative, vocabulary, collaboration, confidence, and AI-consent benefits are product hypotheses awaiting a teacher-led pilot, not measured learning claims.

Compatible Android devices get real WebXR hit-test placement; every modern browser gets the camera-overlay fallback and a deterministic no-camera judge demo.
