## Title

WallAlive — Drawings Come Alive in AR with WebMCP | Hackathon Demo

## Description

WallAlive turns a child’s approved wall drawing into a mathematically reconstructed, closed 3D volume that lives and performs in the room.

Friends join by guest username and paint compact vector strokes on one shared wall. The child explicitly opens the camera and approves one or more drawings. Seven local ONNX graphs isolate the selected figure, preserve artwork, detect semantic parts, and lock movement that lacks enough evidence. A visiting browser agent uses fourteen goal-level WebMCP tools to inspect the room, each rig's verified abilities, and the private Story Passport; prepare an invite without sending it; stage cooperative stories; touch listed 3D quest objects; recommend artwork-aware products; and prepare a creator-credited Shopify handoff. Stories cannot play and draft store files cannot export until a human approves them. Camera access, image pixels, contributor permission, publishing, purchase, and approval are deliberately absent from the tool surface.

The learner-facing result is **Imagine → Sequence → Perform → Reflect**: start from a personally meaningful drawing, shape a beginning–middle–end story, perform it, then retell or revise it. Narrative, vocabulary, collaboration, confidence, and AI-consent benefits are product hypotheses awaiting a teacher-led pilot, not measured learning claims.

Compatible Android devices get real WebXR hit-test placement; every modern browser gets the camera-overlay fallback and a deterministic no-camera judge demo.
